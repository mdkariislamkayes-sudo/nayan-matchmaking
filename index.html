Hadith on Marriage

النِّكَاحُ مِنْ سُنَّتِي فَمَنْ لَمْ يَعْمَلْ بِسُنَّتِي فَلَيْسَ مِنِّي

Bangla: "Marriage is my tradition. Whoever does not act according to my tradition is not from me." (Ibn Majah)
